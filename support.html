How does smart mode work?

Turn on a lullaby and tap the ear button. Lali listens through your iPhone's microphone. When your baby cries, the lullaby gets a little louder to soothe them, then slowly softer once they calm down. It never goes above the maximum volume you choose in Settings → Smart mode. Sound is analyzed right on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

Where should I put my iPhone?

About 2 meters (6 feet) from the crib, with the volume at a medium level. Keep the iPhone on its charger overnight if you use the night light.

Why is smart mode paused when I play on a speaker?

When the sound goes to an AirPlay speaker, your iPhone is usually in another room, so it can't hear your baby. Lali pauses listening and the lullaby keeps playing evenly. Switch the sound back to your iPhone and smart mode resumes.

How do I hear sounds from the night?

Turn on “Sleep sounds” in Settings. Short clips of crying, babble or laughter are saved during sleep. Find them in Statistics → Sleep sounds, where you can play, share as MP3 or delete them.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say “Hey Siri, Lali, play Moon” or “Hey Siri, Lali, stop the lullaby”. You can also add Lalimoon widgets to your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Children's profiles, sleep stats, sleep sounds and voice recordings are part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). Set up your new iPhone from that backup and everything will be there. Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone, so keep your backups on.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Lalimoon with the same Apple ID and tap “Restore Purchases” on the Premium screen. Purchases are linked to your Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Lalimoon, then tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next charge.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored only on your iPhone. Delete clips, recordings or child profiles inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Lalimoon is not a medical device and doesn't replace adult supervision. Always follow safe sleep guidelines.
