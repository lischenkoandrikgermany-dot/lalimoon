Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Lalimoon is a sleep companion for parents. We built it so that everything about your family stays with you. Lalimoon does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Children's profiles — names, birthdays, colors and photos you choose.
  • Sleep records — sleep times, naps, wake-ups, mood after sleep, night feedings and smart-mode reports.
  • Sound clips — if you turn on “Sleep sounds”, short clips of crying, babble or laughter recorded during sleep.
  • Your voice — recordings of a parent's voice you make for soothing.
  • Settings and what you tell Lali in the chat.

This data is stored only in the app on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers — we don't have any. Some of it may be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which are controlled by you and protected by Apple. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. You can also delete clips, recordings and profiles inside the app at any time.

Microphone

Lalimoon uses the microphone only when you turn on smart mode, the hearing check, sleep sounds or a voice recording. Sound is analyzed right on your iPhone by Apple's built-in sound recognition to tell crying from quiet. Audio is never sent anywhere.

Photos

When you add a child's photo, iOS lets you pick a single image. Lalimoon receives only that image and keeps a small copy on your device. The app has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Chat with Lali

Lali's answers are created on your device — by Apple Intelligence where available, or by Lali's built-in knowledge. Your messages are not sent to us or to any third party.

Siri, widgets and purchases

Siri requests, widgets and the Lock Screen are handled by iOS. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to these services: apple.com/legal/privacy.

No ads, no tracking

Lalimoon contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not use your data to track you across apps or websites.

Children

Lalimoon is made for parents and caregivers. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Visit Support.
